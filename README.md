 In this project, I worked with raw e-commerce data and transformed it into meaningful business insights.

What I worked on:
Cleaned and transformed data using Power Query
Built a proper data model with fact and dimension tables
Created DAX measures for Sales, Profit, Profit Margin, Orders, and other KPIs
Analyzed monthly sales and profit trends
Compared Revenue and Profit by Category
Analyzed Sales Channel performance
Studied the relationship between Discount and Profit Margin
Identified Top 10 products by Sales
Performed Customer Segmentation using Sales, Profit, and Order Frequency
Created interactive dashboards with KPIs, charts, slicers, and filters
